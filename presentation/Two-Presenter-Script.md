# Commons: two-member judge pitch

**Presenters:** Bhoomik Sevta and Chitransh Dhakar · B.Tech, 5th semester

**Target length:** about 4 minutes, including the live handoff demo

**Member 1:** Bhoomik · **Member 2:** Chitransh

The PowerPoint includes this script in Presenter View. Keep the demo open before you begin.

## Before presenting

1. Start `start-demo.bat` and click **Reset demo**.
2. Begin in the Northbridge University workspace with the Match board ready.
3. During Slide 4, Bhoomik narrates while Chitransh operates the demo: offer the biology textbooks, switch to **Westview University · Recipient campus**, then accept the incoming offer.
4. If time allows, switch back to Northbridge and mark the handoff fulfilled.

## Slide 1 · Introductions

**Bhoomik:** “Hello, judges and everyone present. I’m Bhoomik Sevta.”

**Chitransh:** “And I’m Chitransh Dhakar. We are B.Tech fifth-semester students, and today we are here to present our idea: Commons.”

## Slide 2 · The gap

**Bhoomik:** “Chitransh, have you ever had things you no longer need taking up space—books, notebooks, stationery, clothes or old gadgets?”

**Chitransh:** “Yes. Sometimes useful things are thrown away or sold as scrap.”

**Bhoomik:** “At the same time, nearby NGOs and community groups may be looking for those very resources.”

**Chitransh:** “Exactly. The problem is the gap between surplus and need.”

## Slide 3 · The solution

**Bhoomik:** “That is the gap Commons aims to fill: a dedicated platform where campus surplus and community needs can be listed together.”

**Chitransh:** “A campus member lists an item. Commons ranks possible matches using category, quantity, condition and known distance. The donor reviews and offers it; the recipient responds, and a coordinator can track the handoff. The match is explainable, and people make the decision.”

## Slide 4 · Live match

**Bhoomik:** “Imagine Northbridge University has 24 biology textbooks in good condition. Westview University needs 12. The sample distance is 3.6 kilometres. Commons surfaces this fit because the category matches and the available quantity covers the request. I’ll send the offer.”

*Chitransh sends the offer, switches the workspace to Westview University, and opens Incoming offers.*

**Chitransh:** “The offer appears for the recipient campus. I’ll accept it. The status changes to Accepted, and after pickup, Northbridge can mark the handoff fulfilled.”

## Slide 5 · How matching works

**Chitransh:** “First, Commons compares the requested category with the listed item. Then it considers quantity, condition and distance when that information is available. The simple algorithm ranks a possible fit and explains why it appeared. A person still decides whether to make or accept the offer.”

**Bhoomik:** “This demo uses fictional sample data. NGO verification and real identity checks are not connected yet.”

## Slide 6 · Pilot plan

**Bhoomik:** “We would start with our campus and three local partners. We would help them onboard, coordinate the early exchanges and learn where the process needs improvement.”

**Chitransh:** “We would track listings, suggested matches, accepted offers and completed handoffs. After the first financial year, we would review the results and decide what to change or expand.”

## Slide 7 · Business model and close

**Bhoomik:** “Our proposed model is brokerage or affiliate revenue: a small fee could apply to suitable paid or non-NGO transactions.”

**Chitransh:** “We could also consider relevant, clearly labelled, non-intrusive third-party promotions. We would test these ideas with partners and keep the community exchange easy to use.”

**Both:** “Commons is about making useful resources visible, connecting them with people who need them, and making each handoff easier to coordinate. Let’s help good things find their next home. Thank you.”

## Short closing option

**Bhoomik:** “A book, a device or a set of supplies can be surplus in one place and exactly what someone else needs.”

**Chitransh:** “Commons helps those two sides find each other. Let’s help good things find their next home. Thank you.”

## If time runs short

Skip marking the handoff fulfilled. The essential demo is **offer → switch campus → accept**. The 3.6 km distance and all sample organisations are illustrative prototype data, not measured results. Describe the business model as a proposal to validate, not established revenue.
